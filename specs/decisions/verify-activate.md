# Decisions: Admin page, Verify and Activate

- The designer starts with Verify and Activate, plus the shared patterns the other tabs reuse (owner gwl, 2026-10-10).
- Every spec leads with **Problem** (one problem, and why the spec exists, answering it) and **Goals and non-goals** (owner, 2026-10-10). This replaced a "Why do we have this spec:" line asked for earlier the same day.
- Choices where the brief was loose (told the owner, 2026-10-10): Undo after Confirm lasts 10 seconds; rows also show the bank transaction number and when the receipt was sent; the plan's start date is the day Activate was pressed; lists move only when Admin asks; a receipt re-sent after a Problem email joins the same row; the Problem email is previewed before sending; bulk Activate goes ahead for the rows that can.
- **Money that doesn't match the plan (OQ-1, owner gwl, 2026-10-10):** every transfer is recorded with the amount Admin saw in the bank, never the plan price. Confirm states the amount it expects; Wrong amount records the typed amount. Too little: the parent is asked for the exact rest, and the top-up joins the same row. Too much: the plan starts and the extra is refunded. Paid twice, or the parent wants the money back: Refund instead. Refunds are worked out from the recorded amounts and can never add up to more than a row received.
  - The owner pointed out that a refund can't simply copy the plan price, because the parent may have mistyped the amount on their bank page (2026-10-10).
- **Ruled out by the owner (2026-10-10), to stop wrong amounts on the parent side:** a Copy button for the amount on the pay page (copy doesn't work on the bank payment page); "Amount you paid" on the receipt form (unnatural for a checkout, heavy friction); a QR with the price filled in (the bank doesn't do that for QR transfers).
- **"Mark sorted" on Verify becomes "Close receipt" (OQ-2, owner gwl, 2026-10-11).** Admin picks a reason (Not paid yet, Wrong receipt, Payment not found, Other). After its 10-second Undo the receipt no longer counts as sent; the parent's page shows the pay page with "We couldn't match your receipt. Send a new one when you've paid." Nothing is emailed. Chosen over keeping "Mark sorted" (the same name as the Reports button, which only tidies a list) and over "Archive" (which suggests nothing else happens). Reports keeps "Mark sorted". Changes the approved `admin-page.md` and `plans-and-payment.md` and adds "Closed receipt" to the glossary.
- **One spec split into three (owner gwl, 2026-10-10),** after a senior engineer's view that 100 rules was too many for one approval: part 1 Check and start, part 2 When something is wrong, part 3 Money that doesn't match. They are approved and built in that order, and all three are still launch day. Rules that repeated another brief became pointers (one fact, one place), and every on-screen word moved into a table in each spec and in `specs/patterns.md`.
- **Copy stays beside the payment code (OQ-3, owner, 2026-10-11).** Admin checks the bank in a web browser and finds the code with Cmd+F, so Copy then paste into Find works. Test sizes gain a laptop screen split in half with the bank's web page.
