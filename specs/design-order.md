The designer writes the detailed rules one area at a time, in this order. The owner approves each area before the next one starts.

## Launch day
1. Admin: Verify and Activate
2. Sign-in and codes (parents and Admin)
3. Admin: Parents (refunds, deletions)
4. Admin: Reports
5. Admin: Answers (To check, Fix feedback)
6. Admin: daily to-do email and test families
7. Parent's payment screens (pay page, receipt form, Subscription activated, tasks paused)
8. Child's pages: Writing Confidence
9. Child's pages: Spelling Confidence
10. Parent page (backup email, sign out everywhere, delete account, Report an issue, reviews)
11. Every page (footer, Terms page, Privacy Notice page, new-task limit message, working with no internet)

## Soon after launch
12. Admin: Download payments
13. Admin: Numbers
14. Admin: Content review
15. Admin: Teaching settings editing
16. Admin: Reviews
17. Admin: Placement history
