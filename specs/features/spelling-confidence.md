# Brief: Spelling Confidence

<!-- project-lead-Opus5.5-agent, 2026-10-04. From the spelling spike (kept privately) and the owner's decisions of 2026-10-04. APPROVED by the owner, 2026-10-04. -->

## Who it's for
A Malaysian child aged 10 (Year 4), practising spelling at home on a phone, and the parent who signs them up. Spelling Confidence is one of two products on Learn English at Puascari.com, beside Writing Confidence (`writing-confidence.md`). Same sign-up and sign-in (a 6-digit code sent to the parent's email, no password), pay page and owner page.

Level: the Malaysian KPM (Ministry of Education) Year 4 standard is the floor. Word list 1 covers it. Word list 2 goes above it. The level target for word list 2 is set in the owner's private notes and lives in the database with the teaching content, never in this public repository.

## The problem
Part 5 of the Year 4 English paper tests spelling: a clue, the first letter, one space per letter, 8 words. Children practise from long printed word lists with no feedback and no help remembering. Words they get wrong are rarely seen again at the right time, and the list runs out with nothing harder after it.

## What should happen

### Start screen
- Header: "Learn English at Puascari.com".
- Title: "Spelling Confidence".
- Line 1: "Spell 8 words per round. Get them right, unlock a bonus round."
- Line 2: "Ready for today's round?"
- Two choices: **1 Fun mode** ("One word at a time. Qualify for the bonus round!") and **2 Exam mode** ("Just like the test paper, marked at the end").
- Up and Down move the selection, Enter picks it. The selected choice fills with colour (no outline).
- No word lists, years, units or levels to choose. The child just starts.

### A round
- 8 words per round, like Part 5 of the paper.
- Each word: a clue, the first letter given, one box per letter.
- Every round mixes lengths: 2 short words (up to 5 letters), 4 middle (6–8), 2 long (9 or more).
- Words come from the whole word pool, never split by school year or unit.
- Fun mode and Exam mode never share the same words in a sitting. Shuffling the order is not enough.
- No teacher notes and no marking while the child works. Everything is marked at the end.

### Fun mode
- A card, one word at a time.
- Help: nothing at first. After 20 seconds on a word, either the hint shows by itself or a Help button appears (picked at random for each word).
- The hint is about meaning, never letters: a word with the same meaning, a word that goes with it, or its opposite (kind picked at random). A hint never gives the spelling away.
- Excitement when the child qualifies for the bonus round (animated stars, "You qualify").

### Exam mode
- Looks like the test paper: a minimal, retro page.
- Questions numbered 25–32, like the real paper. The bonus round is numbered 1–3.
- Typing goes straight in. A chevron marks the current question. Up and Down move between questions, Left and Right between letters.
- Enter asks "Finish Part 5? No | Yes" (and "Mark my paper?" on the bonus page).
- No help of any kind.
- A rubber-stamp effect when the child qualifies.

### Bonus round
- The last button of the round says "Finish", so nothing is given away.
- The child qualifies with no more than half wrong (up to 4 of 8). They then choose "Mark my answers only" or "Let's go, bonus round!". More than half wrong: no bonus, straight to marking.
- 3 questions: pick the right spelling from 4 choices. Words the child got wrong in the round come first.
- Wrong choices are near-miss misspellings only, never real words. The child's own mistake is a choice only if it is 1–2 letters off and not a real word.

### Marking and debrief
- After the round (and the bonus round), everything is marked: spelling score, bonus score and stars.
- Each mistake gets a card: what the child wrote, the right spelling, a short tip, and a Listen button.
- A mistake that is a real word (the right meaning, the wrong word) gets a meaning note instead of a spelling tip.
- Listen plays one simple sentence with the word, read twice. The second reading stresses the word.
- Two recorded voices (British, one female, one male) take turns from card to card. Each debrief starts with a random one. Children never choose a voice.
- Words spelled right are listed under "You spelled these right".
- **Also right** (owner, 2026-10-05): a word can have a short list of other answers that truly fit its clue, first letter and number of boxes, approved by the owner. A correctly spelled "also right" answer counts as right in both modes (score and bonus qualifying). Its debrief card is friendly: "{answer} is right too! The word we were thinking of is {word}", with the Listen sentence for the target word.
- Then: "Practise these words again" or "Next task".

### Remembering words (for each child)
- Every word the child meets sits in a box for that child (Leitner boxes). Right: up one box. Wrong: back to box 1. Right with a hint, or an "also right" answer: stays in the same box.
- Words in lower boxes come back sooner. Words in higher boxes come back later.
- A word that comes back shows a different clue and sentence when one exists (new context). Each word gets 2–3 clues and sentences over time.

### Word list 1 and word list 2
- **Word list 1:** the Year 3 and Year 4 Part 5 spelling words (about 160).
- **Word list 2:** 300 harder words (owner, 2026-10-04). Same task: clue, first letter, boxes, the same length mix and bonus round.
- **When word list 2 starts:** once the child has met every word in list 1 and about 8 in 10 of them are known well (box 3 or higher).
- **After it starts:** across every 5 rounds (40 words), 12 words come from list 1 and 28 from list 2 (30 : 70). Each round has 2 or 3 list 1 words, weakest first.
- **Invisible to the child.** No "list 2", "level up" or level name anywhere. The words simply get harder.
- The owner can add more lists later the same way.

### Plans and prices
- **On its own:** RM39 for 12 months, RM29 for 6 months, or RM11 for 1 month.
- **Included free** with the 12-month Writing Confidence plan (RM59). The 6-month Writing Confidence plan (RM45) does not include it.
- Paid the same way as Writing Confidence: DuitNow QR, payment code, receipt, owner verifies and activates. No plan renews by itself.
- **Free trial (owner, 2026-10-04): 3 rounds only: 2 in Fun mode and 1 in Exam mode.** The trial rounds are fixed: the same words for every trial user, every day. Clearing cookies, a new browser or a VPN gives nothing new, so there is no reason to cheat it.
- Each of the 3 trial rounds has different words (24 in all), so the modes never share words.
- The trial has the full round: help in Fun mode, the bonus round, marking and the debrief with Listen.
- No sign-up for the trial: the child starts at once. The parent signs up with the email code when they pay.
- Nothing is remembered for the child during the trial (no Leitner boxes). New words come only with a plan.
- Refunds follow the writing brief's rule, paid within 30 days of the cancellation.

### Parent
- No spelling progress page. The parent page shows the plan, start date and end date, as for writing.
- The 23-day thank-you email (see the writing brief) gets a few spelling lines: words practised, words known well (box 3 or higher), and words still to practise. Plain words, no levels or list names.
- For spelling, a day counts as active when the child finishes at least one round. A parent with both products gets one email with both parts.

## How it is built (summary)
- One React app with the writing task. Two small requests per round: one to get the round, one to save the answers. Marking, hints and the bonus round run on the phone, so the child never waits.
- The Leitner boxes and the list 1 to list 2 switch are worked out on the server.
- Audio is recorded once, never live, and served from the CDN.
- Words are never edited in place. A changed clue or sentence is saved as a new version, so old answers still match what the child saw.

## Out of scope
Choosing words by year or unit. Choosing a voice. Speaking or handwriting. A spelling progress page. Malay text. Schools and teachers. Lists beyond list 2 (later).
